Model & assumptions

  • Data is simulated, not market quotes.
  • Priced with Black-76 (European exercise) on a single NQ futures price; carry differences between quarterly futures are ignored.
  • Vol surface: SSVI (Gatheral–Jacquier) with equity-style downside skew; time-homogeneous, so each option's smile depends only on time remaining.
  • Rate 4% (discounting only).
  • Calendar: weekly Fridays, monthly 3rd Fridays, quarterly H/M/U/Z; holidays ignored.
  • Theta per calendar day; vega per 1 vol point; $ values use the $20/pt multiplier.
